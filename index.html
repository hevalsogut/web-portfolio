About

Most AI demos stop working the moment they meet real data. I focus on what comes after the demo: ingestion, chunking, retrieval quality, orchestration and evaluation, the engineering that turns a model into a system.

At Light Eagle AG I build multi-agent workflows, RAG pipelines and voice agents for a Swiss firm. Outside work I founded and built Randeviz, an appointment-booking platform for service businesses, and I use mechanistic interpretability to study what happens inside transformer models.

Good AI engineering is 20% models and 80% infrastructure. I build the 80%.
